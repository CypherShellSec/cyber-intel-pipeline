URL: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/new-glassworm-malware-wave-targets-macs-with-trojanized-crypto-wallets/
SUMMARY:
The fourth wave of the GlassWorm campaign targets macOS developers with malicious VSCode/OpenVSX extensions that deliver trojanized versions of crypto wallet applications. The recent attacks use AES-256-CBC–encrypted payloads embedded in compiled JavaScript, and execute after a 15-minute delay to evade analysis. The malware attempts to steal credentials for GitHub, npm, OpenVSX accounts, cryptocurrency wallet data, Keychain passwords, and replaces hardware cryptocurrency wallet apps like Ledger Live and Trezor Suite with trojanized versions. Developers who have installed the affected extensions are recommended to remove them immediately and take additional security measures.
